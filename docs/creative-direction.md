# Creative direction

Lunar Field Log is Akshat Jain’s portfolio framed as a quiet lunar traverse. The opening is composed like an exhibition photograph. A hard-lit astronaut and distant Earth sit against an exposed horizon. Use generous black space and oversized serif type so the identity is legible on arrival. The first decision is between Begin Exploration and View Portfolio.

Explore opens on a compact trail to a communications relay. The vehicle-free, third-person view holds the astronaut low in frame and leaves sky above the ridge. The relay reads by silhouette, with orange reserved for its navigation lamp and the usable interaction. The orbit mark, thin rule and bone typography connect landing, portfolio and discovery. Portfolio Mode uses bone paper and ordinary document navigation. Its first entry is MEETFLIX.

## Design system

Use the existing self-hosted Instrument Serif for editorial headings and Inter for interface and body text. Void #09090b and bone #eeeae2 are the principal values. Copper #f3a776 signals an actionable element. Fine rules and square corners set the rhythm. Use a short entrance fade and a native modal sheet with no entrance delay. On portrait the opener reframes the astronaut above the copy, while Explore uses a closer, centered camera and safe-area-aware thumb controls.

The generated opening plate is the composition reference. The runtime has independent scene assets and its camera, lighting and material treatment are tuned at browser scale.
