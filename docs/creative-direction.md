# Creative direction

Lunar Field Log is an imagined space outpost that visitors can explore on foot. Beauty, immersion and enjoyable exploration guide the design. Scientific references support the world where they help the composition. The original minimal coming-soon page at / retains its approved typography, cream paper and blue accent.

The exploration world uses midnight navy #0b1423, slate #283d57, pale mineral #e9e5dd, copper #e9b486 and cool sky light #a7c8fa. Keep Instrument Serif for editorial headings and Inter for controls and body text. Use warm architectural details to lead the eye through blue shadow. Compose distinct foreground, middle distance and mountain silhouettes around the entire basin. Preserve enough visual quiet to read the astronaut and each landmark.

The surface, site materials, map, discovery panels and direct portfolio share this palette. /experience uses a captured view of the actual browser scene. Its static entry image keeps the initial route lightweight and accurately previews the world. Two scenic artifacts extend the relay and ferry route. The Orrery has delicate copper orbits around a luminous core. The Archive has upright memory fins with warm inset strips.

A controlled orbital camera explains the landscape without moving the astronaut. A contour map shows their position and orientation alongside world-owned locations and current project assignments. Map and overview controls must remain readable in portrait. Discovery is optional. /work and its project URLs are accessible HTML documents.

Movement uses responsive stylized low gravity. Jump takeoff, drift and landing should blend into walking. Flight, planetary travel and the orbital interior belong to future milestones.

The user's supplied cinematic landscape is a composition prompt. Further reference research considered retrofuturistic cities on Pinterest, environment work published on ArtStation, Akufen's Space Advisor on Behance and 21 Hrs on the Moon on Awwwards. The inspected Creator environment by Baptiste Vercier supplies an architectural scale and depth reference. Research images stay outside the shipped site. Some Pinterest and ArtStation pages blocked direct retrieval, so those sources were evaluated from available search previews.
